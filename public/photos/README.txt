PLAYER PHOTOS
-------------
Drop a photo here and it appears on the player's flashcard automatically (no restart needed).
Name the file after the player: lowercase, words joined with hyphens.

   devon-conway.jpg
   virat-kohli.png
   jos-buttler.webp

Accepted formats: jpg, jpeg, png, webp. A portrait photo (about 400 x 520) looks best.
Players without a photo show a designed placeholder with their initials.

Only use photos you have the right to use. Alternatively, put your own hosted image address in
data/players.json as  "photo": "https://..."  on that player.
